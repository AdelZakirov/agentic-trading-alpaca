  * *Asymmetrical Valuation:* At **6.36x forward EPS** and **8.54x EV/EBITDA**, the current price offers a substantial margin of safety if no value-destructive transaction is completed.
* **Near-Term Catalysts:**

  * Upcoming Q2 FY27 earnings release (estimated late October / early November 2026).
  * Continued steady organic FCF generation and debt paydown.
