#### C. Long Put vs. Defined-Risk Put Spread Merit
* **Long Put Disadvantages:** 
  * Following a sharp 20-day -15% selloff and heavy 1.5M volume on the regulatory news, option **implied volatility (IV) is elevated**.
  * Outright long puts suffer from high premium costs, fast theta decay, and IV crush if ENVA chops or stabilizes near the $170 support zone.
* **Defined-Risk Put Spread Advantages (Recommended Bearish Structure):**
  * A **Bear Put Debit Spread (e.g., Buying $170 Put / Selling $150 Put)** or **Bear Call Credit Spread (e.g., Selling $185 Call / Buying $200 Call)** is vastly superior.
  * **Merit:** It offsets the high IV skew, lowers net capital outlay, protects against sideways consolidation/mean-reversion, and caps downside risk while offering attractive 2:1 to 3:1 reward-to-risk profiles over a 1–4 week horizon.
