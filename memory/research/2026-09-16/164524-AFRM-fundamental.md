## 5. Strategic & Technical Assessment

### A. Earnings Quality & Normalized Profitability
* **Sourced Fact:** AFRM recorded FY26 GAAP net income of $1.93B ($5.53 EPS), driven by a $1.44B net tax provision benefit (reversal/recognition of deferred tax assets). Pretax income stood at $492.73M and operating income at $870.99M.
* **Analyst Judgment:** Core operating performance has genuinely achieved GAAP operating profitability ($871M vs $(265M) two years ago). However, investors must look through the optical headline trailing P/E of 13.1x; underlying normalized earnings power places current year P/E closer to 16x–18x, which remains reasonable for 30%+ topline expansion.

### B. Credit Performance & Funding Sensitivity
* **Sourced Fact:** Provision for credit losses increased to $796.65M in FY26 from $616.68M in FY25 (remaining controlled at ~18.7% of revenue). Affirm holds $10.02B in debt against $1.70B in unrestricted cash.
* **Analyst Judgment:** Affirm's proprietary underwriting models (RL-based credit decisioning at transaction level) continue to perform well within historical delinquency bands. Because Affirm originates short-duration loans (average duration <6 months), liability pricing resets rapidly. A flat or declining interest rate environment serves as a dual catalyst by lowering cost of funds and expanding secondary market demand for Affirm ABS.

### C. Growth Durability & Competitive Moat
* Affirm's penetration across major enterprise checkout workflows (Amazon, Shopify, Walmart, Apple ecosystem) and the scaling of the Affirm Card drive high recurring transaction frequency. Merchant network effects (+51% active merchants YoY to 571k) insulate Affirm from generic point-of-sale entrants.
