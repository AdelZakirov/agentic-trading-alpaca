#### C. Conceptual Comparison: Outright Put vs. Bear Put Spread
* **Outright Long Put:**
  * *Disadvantages:* Highly vulnerable to post-earnings implied volatility (IV) crush and accelerated theta decay over a 1–10 day duration. Buying puts *after* a >15% gap-down requires substantial further downside just to overcome option premium costs.
* **Bear Put Spread (e.g., Long ATM/Slightly OTM Put + Short Lower OTM Put):**
  * *Advantages:* **Conceptually superior for defined-risk bearish exposure.** Selling the lower strike put helps finance the long put, offsets elevated implied volatility, and caps maximum loss while targeting realistic downside follow-through (e.g., toward $50.00–$51.00 support).
