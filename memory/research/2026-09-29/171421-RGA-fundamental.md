#### B. Accounting Clarity: Cash Flow vs. Distributable Earnings
* *Crucial Reinsurance Principle:* Insurer operating cash flow ($5.20B) and reported generic free cash flow ($9.84B) should **not** be treated as ordinary corporate distributable earnings. 
* These metrics reflect statutory policyholder deposit inflows, collateral movements, premium floats, and reserve buildups required to match long-dated liabilities. Distributable capital is properly measured via statutory capital generation, holding company excess liquidity, and operating ROE on tangible equity.
