#### B. Underwriting & Catastrophe Exposure Profile
* Allstate has a significantly higher proportion of personal auto and homeowners lines than commercial multi-line carriers like Chubb or Travelers. Homeowners coverage exposes ALL directly to secondary peril events (severe convective storms, hail, inland wind) and hurricane landfall risk. While prior auto rate approvals fixed combined ratios in early 2026, homeowners margins remain highly vulnerable to volatile catastrophe frequency.

#### C. Quality of Insurance Cash-Flow Metrics
* **Accounting Nuance:** While reported Free Cash Flow is high ($15.16B) and TTM Operating Cash Flow sits at $12.47B, standard industrial FCF calculations are deceptive for insurance companies.
* Insurance operating cash flow includes upfront premium collections (float) before future claims are settled. These funds are legally and regulatorily restricted under statutory Risk-Based Capital (RBC) frameworks and must back policyholder reserves; they cannot be treated as discretionary free cash flow for unrestrained dividends or buybacks.
