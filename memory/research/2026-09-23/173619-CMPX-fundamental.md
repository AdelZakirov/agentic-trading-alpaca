### 5. In-Depth Investigation & Trade Analysis

#### A. The Exact Clinical/Regulatory Event
* **Event & Timing:** In early September 2026, Compass released FDA regulatory feedback regarding its lead bispecific antibody **tovecimig (CTX-009)** in second-line biliary tract cancer (BTC).
* **The Root Cause:** While the Phase 2/3 (COMPASS-04) trial demonstrated positive objective response rate (ORR) and progression-free survival (PFS), it failed to establish a statistically significant **overall survival (OS)** benefit. 
* **The Escalation:** The FDA formally recommended that Compass conduct an additional randomized trial demonstrating OS benefit *before* submitting a Biologics License Application (BLA). Compass management announced it disagreed and intended to proceed with the BLA filing regardless. The Street interpreted this confrontational regulatory path as carrying a severe risk of a **Refusal to File (RTF)** or **Complete Response Letter (CRL)**.
