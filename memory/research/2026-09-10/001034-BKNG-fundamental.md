* **Geographic Mix:** Booking’s high exposure to Europe and APAC acts as a defensive buffer against localized U.S. domestic air/hotel normalization.
* **Direct Channel Power:** Over 55% of room nights are booked directly through Booking.com apps, mitigating search engine ad inflation and customer acquisition cost (CAC) pressures.
