### 5. In-Depth Strategic & Cycle Analysis

#### A. Revenue & Profitability Quality
* **High Operating Leverage:** Unlike pure balance-sheet balance lenders, Figure operates primarily as a capital-efficient blockchain distribution platform (Figure Connect). Revenues are generated via origination technology fees and marketplace liquidity spreads.
* **Cash Flow Divergence:** While GAAP net margin is 38.3%, operating cash flow remains negative (-$86.5M) due to intermediate funding facilities before loan syndication. True cash conversion will depend on warehouse turnaround velocity.

#### B. Credit & Macro Fintech Cycle Exposure
* **Sensitivity to Home Equity & Interest Rates:** FIGR's core franchise is tokenized HELOCs and second-lien mortgages. Lower or fluctuating interest rates increase refinancing and home equity borrowing demand; however, a macro housing correction or rising delinquency rates could chill secondary-market liquidity on Figure Connect.

#### C. Post-Listing Supply & Insider Risk
* **1-Year IPO Anniversary (September 2025 – September 2026):** With the initial public listing dating back to September 2025, major lockup expirations have matured. 
* **Ownership Profile:** Institutional ownership is concentrated at **76.4%**, while insider ownership is **6.33%**. Short interest is moderate at **6.66% of float** (3.33 days to cover), limiting immediate squeeze risk while indicating standard institutional liquidity.
