### 4. Vehicle Comparison: Long Put vs. Defined-Risk Bear Put Spread
For a 1-week horizon following a clinical-trial gap:

| Feature | Long Put (Single-Leg) | Bear Put Spread (Vertical Debit Spread) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **IV Impact** | **Severe Drag:** Peak IV post-announcement will rapidly crush, causing heavy theta/vega loss even if the stock chops sideways. | **Mitigated:** Selling the lower strike absorbs elevated IV and offsets vega/theta decay. |
| **Risk/Reward** | Defined risk, but expensive premium requires an immediate, violent secondary drop to break even. | Defined risk with lower capital outlay, higher probability of profit on a moderate move. |
| **Verdict** | **Avoid.** High risk of IV crush over 1 week. | **Recommended.** E.g., Buy $15 Put / Sell $13 Put (or $14/$12 Put) to capture downside continuation to $13.00–$13.80 while mitigating IV collapse. |
