#### A. Exact Catalyst & Root Cause of 46% Drop on 13.5x Volume
* **Date:** September 29, 2026.
* **Catalyst:** uniQure disclosed updated 48-month Phase I/II clinical data for AMT-130 in Huntington's disease.
* **Mechanism:** Although 3-year data previously showed significant clinical slowing, the 4-year follow-up exhibited a loss of statistical significance (44% slowing, $p > 0.05$ on primary functional composite metrics) and an apparent deceleration in therapeutic effect size over extended duration. This triggered capitulation from institutional holders who had priced in a near-frictionless accelerated BLA approval.

#### B. Invalidation of Prior Regulatory Assumptions
* **Previous Assumption:** FDA alignment achieved in mid-2026 suggested uniQure could submit a BLA based entirely on Phase I/II data vs. natural history controls under the Accelerated Approval pathway.
* **Current Status:** The core filing assumption is severely challenged. While uniQure can still submit the BLA based on the 3-year cutoff, the FDA review division and Advisory Committee (AdCom) will scrutinize the lack of 4-year statistical significance. This increases the probability of:
  1. A Complete Response Letter (CRL) requesting a randomized Phase III confirmatory study before approval, or
  2. Substantial delays pushing commercialization out past 2028.

* **Financing Risk Assessment:** **Low near-term dilution risk**. uniQure is not under immediate pressure to execute a distressed equity offering. However, long-term financing risk hinges on servicing $552.17M in total debt if AMT-130 fails to reach commercial cash generation.
